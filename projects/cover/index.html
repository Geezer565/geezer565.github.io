EMBODIED AI · HUMAN–ROBOT INTERACTION

机械臂协作中的意图推断与主动澄清Intent Inference and Active Clarification for Collaborative Manipulation

面向信息不完整的人机协作场景，研究机器人如何识别缺失条件、预测候选动作后果，并通过主动提问降低误操作风险。

Research on how a robot can recognize missing evidence, predict the consequences of candidate actions, and ask targeted questions before acting in collaborative manipulation.

RealMan RM65-BMultimodal StateActive ClarificationCounterfactual Evaluation
01

研究问题Research Question

当用户指令含糊或视觉信息不完整时，机器人需要先判断“缺了什么”，而不是直接执行一个可能错误的动作。

When instructions are ambiguous or visual evidence is incomplete, the robot should identify what is missing before committing to a potentially unsafe action.

02

方法框架Method

围绕多模态状态建模、潜在空间预测、反事实动作评估与主动澄清构建闭环，并利用用户反馈更新后续决策。

The loop combines multimodal state modeling, latent prediction, counterfactual action evaluation, and active clarification, then updates decisions using user feedback.

03

实机验证Robot Validation

方法在 RealMan RM65-B 机械臂协作平台上进行仿真与实机验证，覆盖动态避障、视觉伺服和抓取修正等环节。

The method is evaluated in simulation and on a RealMan RM65-B platform, including dynamic avoidance, visual servoing, and grasp correction.
