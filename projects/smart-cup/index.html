EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · TEAM PROJECT

ESP32 物联网智能水杯ESP32 Smart Cup

带领团队围绕日常饮水场景开发智能水杯，把设备端感知、语音交互和移动端连接起来，实现动态监测与喝水提醒。

Led a team project for a connected cup that brings together device-side sensing, voice interaction, and mobile access for monitoring and hydration reminders.

ESP32VoiceWi‑FiBluetooth团队项目Team Project

项目如何工作How the system works

项目以 ESP32 为设备端核心：水杯提供动态监测和喝水提醒，并通过语音、小程序 Wi‑Fi 和 App 蓝牙三种方式与用户交互。重点在于把设备、无线连接与提醒体验串成完整的使用流程。

The ESP32 anchors the device side. The cup supports monitoring and hydration reminders, with voice, Wi‑Fi mini-program, and Bluetooth app interactions. The work connects the device, wireless links, and reminder experience into one user flow.

设备端监测Device monitoring
Wi‑Fi / 蓝牙连接Wi‑Fi / Bluetooth link
语音与移动端交互Voice and mobile interaction
喝水提醒Hydration reminder
01 · DEVICE

嵌入式设备Embedded device

基于 ESP32 组织设备端功能，将动态监测与提醒逻辑放进智能水杯的使用场景。

Used ESP32 for the device-side functions, connecting monitoring and reminder behavior to the cup's everyday use.

02 · INTERACTION

多端交互Multiple interfaces

语音、小程序 Wi‑Fi 与 App 蓝牙提供不同的交互入口，覆盖设备与手机之间的连接。

Voice, Wi‑Fi mini-program, and Bluetooth app offered different ways to interact with the device.

03 · OUTCOME

竞赛成果Competition outcome

团队项目获得“毛宗源杯”智能控制设计大赛优胜奖。

The team project received a Merit Award in the Mao Zongyuan Cup Intelligent Control Design Competition.
