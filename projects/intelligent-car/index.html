NATIONAL COMPETITION · TEAM PROJECT

第二十一届全国大学生智能汽车竞赛地瓜机器人创意组智能车21st National Intelligent Car Competition — D-Robotics Creative Division

面向“智慧医疗”赛题，团队构建了由视觉—激光雷达融合、局部目标点预测、底盘控制与任务状态机组成的 ROS 2 自主智能车。

For the “Smart Healthcare” challenge, the team built a ROS 2 autonomous vehicle combining camera–LiDAR fusion, local waypoint prediction, chassis control, and a mission state machine.

National Finals Second PrizeSouth China Regional First PrizeRDK X5ROS 2 HumbleCamera–LiDAR
01

感知与融合Perception and Fusion

USB 相机与 N10 激光雷达提供环境信息，图像—雷达融合网络输出局部目标点，为控制层提供可执行的空间参考。

A USB camera and N10 LiDAR provide scene information. The image–radar fusion network predicts a local waypoint for the controller.

02

控制与底盘Control and Chassis

上位机生成速度与转向指令，STM32 驱动 Ackermann 底盘并回传车速、惯导和电压等状态。

The upper computer generates speed and steering commands. An STM32 drives the Ackermann chassis and returns speed, IMU, and voltage states.

03

任务系统Mission System

任务状态机组织顺逆时针绕行、返回、停车与语义任务切换，并将感知、控制和执行串成完整链路。

A mission state machine coordinates clockwise and counterclockwise navigation, return, stop, and semantic-task transitions.
