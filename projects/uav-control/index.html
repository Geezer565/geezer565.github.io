AUTONOMOUS SYSTEMS · ROBUST CONTROL

无人机动力学与鲁棒控制UAV Dynamics and Robust Control

针对四旋翼系统的非线性、欠驱动与强耦合特性，研究模型不确定、外界扰动和时变任务条件下的稳定轨迹跟踪。

Research on stable trajectory tracking for nonlinear, underactuated, and strongly coupled quadrotors under model uncertainty, external disturbances, and time-varying tasks.

6-DoF DynamicsRobust ControlAdaptive ControlRBF NetworksMATLAB / Simulink
01

系统建模System Modeling

建立六自由度动力学模型，分析位置环、姿态环、参数不确定性与外部扰动之间的耦合关系。

A six-DoF model captures the coupling among position, attitude, parameter uncertainty, and external disturbances.

02

控制与辨识Control and Identification

关注 Lyapunov 稳定性分析、反步与滑模控制、自适应律、RBF 神经网络未知项逼近以及有限时间与预设性能控制。

The work studies Lyapunov analysis, backstepping and sliding-mode control, adaptive laws, RBF approximation, finite-time control, and prescribed performance.

03

仿真验证Simulation

使用 MATLAB/Simulink 检查轨迹跟踪误差、控制输入、参数估计收敛性与扰动抑制能力。

MATLAB/Simulink is used to evaluate tracking error, control inputs, parameter-estimation convergence, and disturbance rejection.
