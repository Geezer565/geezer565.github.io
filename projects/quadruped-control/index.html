ROBOT CONTROL · ONGOING STUDY

四足机器人运动控制Quadruped Robot Control

围绕宇树 GO2 的动力学建模与运动控制，研究复杂地形、外部扰动和模型误差下的稳定运动方法。

Ongoing study of dynamics and locomotion control for Unitree GO2 under rough terrain, external disturbances, and modeling uncertainty.

Unitree GO2SRBDMPC / NMPCWBC / WBICROS 2
01

动力学建模Dynamics

从单刚体动力学与质心动力学出发，连接接触力、机体状态、落足约束与可行运动范围。

Starting from single-rigid-body and centroidal dynamics, the work connects contact forces, body state, foothold constraints, and feasible motion.

02

控制方法Control Stack

重点关注 MPC、NMPC、MPPI 与 WBC/WBIC 的分层组合，并跟踪 CLF/CBF、鲁棒补偿和自适应控制方法。

The focus is hierarchical combinations of MPC, NMPC, MPPI, and WBC/WBIC, together with CLF/CBF, robust compensation, and adaptive control.

03

验证环境Validation

通过 MuJoCo、Gazebo 和 ROS 2 搭建复现与验证环境，用于检查轨迹跟踪、接触稳定性和扰动响应。

MuJoCo, Gazebo, and ROS 2 are used for reproduction and validation of tracking, contact stability, and disturbance response.
