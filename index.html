第二十一届全国大学生智能汽车竞赛地瓜机器人创意组智能车21st National Intelligent Car Competition — D-Robotics Creative Division

面向全国总决赛“智慧医疗”赛题，团队基于 OriginCar、RDK X5、STM32、USB 相机与 N10 激光雷达构建 ROS 2 自主智能车。图像—雷达融合模型预测局部目标点，控制器生成速度与转向指令，STM32 驱动 Ackermann 底盘并回传车速、惯导与电压；任务状态机完成顺逆时针绕行、返回、停车与语义任务切换。Built a ROS 2 autonomous vehicle for the “Smart Healthcare” task using OriginCar, RDK X5, STM32, a USB camera, and N10 LiDAR. A camera–LiDAR fusion model predicts local waypoints; the controller generates speed and steering commands, while the STM32 drives the Ackermann chassis and reports vehicle state. A task-state machine manages clockwise and counterclockwise navigation, return, stop, and semantic-task transitions.

Road to ChatGPT：自然语言处理与情感分析Road to ChatGPT: NLP and Sentiment Analysis

在新加坡国立大学“人工智能与 ChatGPT”项目中，基于 IMDB 电影评论数据构建英文文本情感分类模型，完成文本向量化、词嵌入、神经网络训练与对比实验；围绕学习率、优化器、Embedding 维度、网络结构、Dropout 与 L2 正则化进行调优，测试准确率达到 88.7%。同时共同完成 AI 在无人机控制中的应用调研与英文汇报，涵盖 RBF 神经网络扰动估计、强化学习控制及工程应用风险。Built an English sentiment-classification model on the IMDB reviews dataset during NUS's Artificial Intelligence & ChatGPT program. Conducted comparative experiments on text vectorization, word embeddings, learning rate, optimizers, network depth, dropout, and L2 regularization, reaching 88.7% test accuracy. Also co-presented a study of AI for UAV control, covering RBF-based disturbance estimation, reinforcement-learning control, and deployment risks.

TensorFlow · NLP · Word Embeddings · Sentiment Analysis · Model Optimization · Test Accuracy 88.7%
