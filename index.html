My interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence and robotic systems, spanning collaborative manipulation, quadruped control, robust UAV control, multimodal perception, and LLM applications.

I aim to build complete technical pipelines—from dynamics and learning methods, through simulation and software development, to validation on embedded devices and physical robots.
